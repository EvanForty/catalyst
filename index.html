faq.txt · Notepad_□×
What is CATALYST?

An AI-powered launchpad that turns a text prompt into a fully branded, deployable Solana token project: name, ticker, logo, banner, description, metadata, website and launch content.

How is it different from other launchpads?

Most launchpads handle deployment. CATALYST covers the whole project lifecycle, from the idea and branding to the website and the first posts.

Can I change what it makes?

Yes. Ask in plain words: change colors, redesign the logo, rename it or adjust token settings, without starting over.

Who holds my keys?

You do. CATALYST prepares the transaction; your wallet signs it. It never takes custody of your keys or tokens.

How do fees work?

Under the proposed model, 50% of protocol fees buy $CATALYST, 30% fund platform development and 20% go to ecosystem incentives.

When does it launch?

Launch details, the $CATALYST contract address and on-chain deploys are announced on X at @CatalystFun.
